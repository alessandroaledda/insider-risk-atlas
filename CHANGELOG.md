## 1.0, 25 October 2026

Named Insider Risk Atlas before the first sharing: the name Insider Threat Exposure Map (ITEM) stood too close to Insider Threat Matrix (ITM) for a work that uses the Matrix as its reference; the identifiers take the prefix ATLAS (ATLAS-001 to ATLAS-039); the repository is insider-risk-atlas and the page is at /atlas/. The release shared with Forscie Limited for comments on the mapping, unlisted until the public announcement. The content of 0.6, with ITEM-005, ITEM-015, and ITEM-031 reworded and the references reconciled with the Matrix repository at tag v2.13.0. The versions before 1.0 were internal iterations; from 1.0 the version that counts is the released one, and every correction is a later, dated version.

## 0.6, 25 October 2026

After a second independent review, of form and content. The act Sabotage is named Disruption, a grouping that includes unintended impairment; the six combinations and the eight populations redefined (exfiltration as transfer or disclosure beyond the authorized handling boundary, fraud as diversion through deception or misuse of entrusted access or authority, misconduct towards people as the unauthorized access or use of personal data). The primary assignment rule and the intent convention stated in the guide; the assignment of ITEM-039 revised from misconduct towards people to exfiltration of personal data (Exfiltration 18, Misconduct 5). Thirty-five scenarios corrected in their sentence, signals, or controls; the negligent scenarios state the mistaken assumption that makes them negligent; the agent scenarios state the owner's configuration or oversight error; ITEM-038 records its focal actor. Matrix references revised: 40 distinct entries (MT005, MT015, IF001.002, IF002.001 on ITEM-007, IF014.005 on ITEM-011, IF016.005, IF016.013, IF018.002, IF019, IF022.005, IF022.006 on ITEM-029, IF044.005, IF045.002 on ITEM-039 removed; IF001.001, IF010.001, IF044.001, IF001.006 added), with a rationale where a reference is conditional. Reporting obligations rewritten as a reporting assessment by asset, with the conditions of each instrument and links to the texts (GDPR, MAR, NIS2, DORA and Delegated Regulation 2025/301). The coverage table restated with a status per section (direct, partial, contextual, outside the model): 31 sections with a direct or partial mapping, 14 without a primary mapping. On the page: the revision date and a self-link on every card, the context of the card at every width, a visible selection status above the drawing, the act counts beside their labels, darker idle lines, signals as a list, navigation state kept across filters, cards, and history, the cards readable without a script, a print rule, a citation line, and the review status stated.

Entry titles and detection and prevention counts reconciled with the Matrix repository at tag v2.13.0 (commit b0c1502) on 25 October 2026: 61 entries identical. Also in this version: The map is named Insider Threat Exposure Map (ITEM); scenario identifiers take the prefix ITEM (ITEM-001 to ITEM-039), replacing IRM, which reads as Microsoft Purview Insider Risk Management. The repository is named insider-threat-exposure-map, consistent with insider-threat-risk-architecture and insider-threat-event-requirements; the page on the site is at /item/, consistent with /intra/ and /iter/.

## 0.5, 24 October 2026

After an independent review. Reporting obligations rewritten against the acts: GDPR articles 33 and 34 with the awareness clock and the exceptions, the Market Abuse Regulation insider list under article 18, the NIS2 and DORA clocks stated separately, the ITER types corrected. Eleven Matrix attributions removed where the scenario did not state the behaviour the entry describes (Insider Trading, Access to a Retained Former Account, Deliberate Safety Control Defeat, Physical Sabotage, Weakening Controls For a Third Party, AI Agent Reconnaissance and Privilege Exploitation on negligent agents, Malicious Code Contribution, Personnel Diversion, Illicit Access to a Privileged Account, Intellectual Property Theft on forwarded board material): 47 distinct entries. Containment steps restated as actions that stop the act; preservation moved to response. Acronyms spelled out; detections and preventions written in full; the coverage table distinguishes assignment to a row from citation by a scenario (16 of 31 assigned sections cited).
