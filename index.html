Insider risk management

Insider Risk Map

An organizational layer for the Insider Threat Matrix™: what is done to which asset, by who has the access.

The Insider Threat Matrix™ classifies what an insider does, by technique. A program has to answer two questions before it can choose its use cases and write its agreements: who, in this organization, has the access, and which asset the act reaches. The rows combine the act with the asset; the columns are populations defined by what their access reaches. Select a cell: the scenarios open below, each with its signals, the chain of controls that answers it, its Insider Threat Matrix™ entries, and the reporting duty that follows from the asset.

Scenarios in the cell Selected Deliberate Negligent

How to read it

The rows are the act and the asset together. A motive does not change what is observed or what follows: the client list leaves the organization the same way whether it is sold, carried to the next job, or printed to be worked on at home. The act and the asset do change both: data leaving is seen on its movement and answered with evidence of what left; a system damaged is seen on changes and answered with a restore; money diverted is seen in the business application and answered with a hold; a person harmed is seen on access to records and answered through the Data Protection Officer. Intent, deliberate or negligent, is a property of the scenario: the same alert, a different consequence and a different lever.

The columns are populations defined by what their access reaches, not by the department's name, so that the same map fits a bank, a utility or a manufacturer. An organization maps its own roles onto them.

The Insider Threat Matrix™ entries carry the techniques. Each scenario points to the ITM motives, means and infringements that describe it, down to the channel; the map adds no technique of its own. Detections and preventions per technique are the Matrix's; the scenario is where an organization decides which of them it needs.

Out of scope by definition: an external attacker using a stolen identity, which is the work of security operations. A colleague using another colleague's identity is inside the map: the act is theirs, the identity is the channel.

What an organization adds on its copy: which scenarios apply and which it has seen; for each, whether the act is visible and whether the asset is recognized; the lever chosen: governance, technical, awareness, or the hand-over to another function. The map does not measure anyone's coverage; the copy does.

Where each of the 45 Infringement sections of the Matrix sits on this map, and the 14 the map excludes and why. The mapping is pinned to this version of the Matrix; a new version re-runs the table.

Excluded, with the reason

IF007 Unlawfully Accessing Copyrighted Material: Conduct towards third parties' rights, not an act on the organization's assets; acceptable-use matter.

IF008 Inappropriate Web Browsing: Conduct; the monitoring of browsing content is restricted under European employment law and is not a program measure.

IF009 Installing Unapproved Software: Endpoint hygiene, handled by security operations; appears in scenarios as a means, not as the act.

IF012 Public Statements Resulting in Brand Damage: Communications and reputation; not an act on an information asset; the communications and HR functions.

IF017 Excessive Personal Use: Conduct; a program measure here would fail the proportionality test in every European jurisdiction.

IF019 Non-Corporate Device: A means (the device), not an act; appears in scenarios as the channel.

IF020 Unauthorized VPN Client: A means; network hygiene, handled by security operations.

IF023 Regulatory Non-Compliance: Export, sanctions and competition law: the trade compliance function's domain; an interface, not a program measure.

IF025 Internal Credential Sharing: A means: the colleague's identity as the channel; the act performed with it sits in its own row.

IF031 Unauthorized Presence in Restricted Physical Areas: Physical security's domain; a preparation for sabotage or theft, handled at the interface with corporate security.

IF035 Unauthorized Work Location: Conduct and compliance (tax, immigration, data residency); location monitoring is restricted and is not a program measure.

IF038 Undisclosed Concurrent Employment: Conflict of interest and conduct; the HR and compliance functions.

IF039 Unauthorized Account Access: A means: access to another account; the act performed with it sits in its own row.

IF041 Unauthorized Organizational Representation: Conduct and legal exposure; where a contract or payment follows, the fraud row applies.

Means, motives, preparation and anti-forensics are properties of a scenario and are cited inside the scenarios, not placed on the grid.
