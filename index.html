Insider risk management

Insider Risk Map

A layer above the Insider Threat Matrix™ for the people who build the controls: what is done, to which asset, by whom.

The Insider Threat Matrix™ is an open framework for computer-enabled insider threat investigations. It describes insider activity in five articles, motive, means, preparation, infringement, and anti-forensics, so that an event can be reconstructed from its first step to its aftermath. Its unit is the entry. The Insider Risk Map is a layer above it, for the people who design, build, and keep the controls: security architects, detection engineers, the insider threat function. Its unit is the scenario, placed where an organization can act on it. The rows are the act and the asset it reaches; the columns are the populations defined by what their access reaches. Select a cell: its scenarios open below, each with the signals that make it observable, the chain of controls that answers it, the Matrix entries that describe its techniques, and the reporting obligations to be assessed for that asset under European law.

Reading the map

A row is an act on an asset. Six rows, because six combinations change what is observed and what follows. Data leaving is seen on its movement and answered with the evidence of what left. A system damaged is seen on changes and destructive actions and answered with a restore. Records altered are seen on writes outside the process and answered by restoring the version. Money diverted is seen in the business application, and answered with a payment hold. A person harmed is seen on access to records without a business reason and answered through the Data Protection Officer and the person’s rights. The motive is a property of the scenario, not of the row: a client list leaves the organization the same way whether it is sold, carried to the next employer, or printed to be worked on at home. The motive changes the consequence; the act and the asset change the controls.

Intent is a property of the scenario, not of the row. Negligent, on this map, means no intent to harm: the act is meant, the exposure is not. Deliberate or negligent, the alert is often the same; the consequence and the lever differ. A cell shows both counts, deliberate above the diagonal and negligent below.

A column is a population defined by what its access reaches. A department name would tie the map to one organization; the reach of the access makes one map fit a bank, a utility, and a manufacturer. The populations overlap, and an organization maps its own roles to them. Customer-facing access reaches one customer at a time, and a bulk export from it is a signal in itself; data and analytics access reaches the whole base in one query: two risks with different signals. Non-human identities, agents and automations acting under their own identity, are a population because their access is real and their principal is not always visible.

The Matrix carries the techniques. Every scenario points to the Insider Threat Matrix™ entries that describe it, motives, means, preparations, and infringements, down to the channel where the scenario states one, with the number of detections and preventions the Matrix holds for each. The technique reference is the Matrix’s; the map holds the scenario. Where an infringement of the Matrix is absent from the grid, the coverage table says why: conduct whose monitoring is restricted by European employment and data protection law, a means rather than an act, or another function’s domain. An entry absent from the grid can still be cited by a scenario.

Scope. The map covers acts by people and identities with legitimate access, including a colleague using another colleague’s identity: the act is theirs, the identity is the channel. An external attacker using a stolen identity belongs to security operations.

What an organization adds on its copy: which scenarios apply and which it has seen; for each, whether the act is visible and whether the asset is recognized; the lever it chose: governance, technical, awareness, or referral to another function. Coverage is measured on the copy.

Coverage of the Insider Threat Matrix™ infringements, version 2.13.0

Where each of the 45 Infringement sections of the Matrix sits on this map, and the 14 the map excludes and why. The mapping is pinned to this version of the Matrix; a new version of the Matrix requires the table to be reviewed. Assignment to a row is a classification; 16 of the 31 assigned sections are cited by at least one scenario in this version. An exclusion from the grid does not exclude an entry from a scenario's references: IF019 and IF039 are cited as means.

Assigned to rows

Exfiltration, personal data: IF001 Exfiltration via Web Service, IF002 Exfiltration via Physical Medium, IF003 Exfiltration via Media Capture, IF004 Exfiltration via Other Network Medium, IF005 Exfiltration via Messaging Applications, IF006 Unauthorized Printing of Documents, IF010 Exfiltration via Email, IF018 Sharing on AI Chatbot Platforms, IF022 Data Loss, IF024 Exfiltration via Screen Sharing, IF027 Installing Malicious Software, IF028 Delegated Execution via Artificial Intelligence Agents, IF030 Exfiltration via SMS/MMS, IF034 Exfiltration via Automated Transcription.

Exfiltration, business information: IF001 Exfiltration via Web Service, IF002 Exfiltration via Physical Medium, IF003 Exfiltration via Media Capture, IF004 Exfiltration via Other Network Medium, IF005 Exfiltration via Messaging Applications, IF006 Unauthorized Printing of Documents, IF010 Exfiltration via Email, IF011 Providing Access to a Unauthorized Third Party, IF016 Misappropriation of Funds, IF018 Sharing on AI Chatbot Platforms, IF022 Data Loss, IF024 Exfiltration via Screen Sharing, IF028 Delegated Execution via Artificial Intelligence Agents, IF030 Exfiltration via SMS/MMS, IF032 External Credential Sharing, IF034 Exfiltration via Automated Transcription, IF045 Unauthorized Data Access.

Sabotage, systems and operations: IF011 Providing Access to a Unauthorized Third Party, IF013 Disruption of Business Operations, IF014 Unauthorized Changes to IT Systems, IF022 Data Loss, IF026 Denial of Service, IF027 Installing Malicious Software, IF028 Delegated Execution via Artificial Intelligence Agents, IF029 Codebase Integrity Compromise, IF032 External Credential Sharing, IF033 Digital Defacement, IF037 Physical Sabotage, IF042 Deliberate Safety Control Defeat.

Sabotage, data integrity: IF040 Data Integrity Manipulation.

Fraud, money and physical assets: IF015 Theft, IF016 Misappropriation of Funds, IF043 Organizational Resource Diversion, IF044 Abuse of Decision-Making Authority.

Misconduct towards people: IF021 Harassment and Discrimination, IF028 Delegated Execution via Artificial Intelligence Agents, IF036 Misuse of Corporate Communication Channels, IF045 Unauthorized Data Access.

Excluded from the grid, with the reason

IF007 Unlawfully Accessing Copyrighted Material: Conduct towards third parties' rights, not an act on the organization's assets; acceptable-use matter.

IF008 Inappropriate Web Browsing: Conduct; the monitoring of browsing content is restricted under European employment law and is not a program measure.

IF009 Installing Unapproved Software: Endpoint hygiene, handled by security operations; appears in scenarios as a means, not as the act.

IF012 Public Statements Resulting in Brand Damage: Communications and reputation; not an act on an information asset; the communications and Human Resources functions.

IF017 Excessive Personal Use: Conduct; a program measure here would have to pass the necessity and proportionality test that European employment and data protection law set for monitoring, and is unlikely to.

IF019 Non-Corporate Device: A means (the device), not an act; appears in scenarios as the channel.

IF020 Unauthorized VPN Client: A means; network hygiene, handled by security operations.

IF023 Regulatory Non-Compliance: Export, sanctions, and competition law: the trade compliance function's domain; an interface, not a program measure.

IF025 Internal Credential Sharing: A means: the colleague's identity as the channel; the act performed with it sits in its own row.

IF031 Unauthorized Presence in Restricted Physical Areas: Physical security's domain; a preparation for sabotage or theft, handled at the interface with corporate security.

IF035 Unauthorized Work Location: Conduct and compliance (tax, immigration, and data residency); location monitoring is restricted and is not a program measure.

IF038 Undisclosed Concurrent Employment: Conflict of interest and conduct; the HR and compliance functions.

IF039 Unauthorized Account Access: A means: access to another account, or to one's own after it should have been closed; the act performed with it sits in its own row.

IF041 Unauthorized Organizational Representation: Conduct and legal exposure; where a contract or payment follows, the fraud row applies.

Means, motives, preparation, and anti-forensics are properties of a scenario and are cited inside the scenarios, not placed on the grid.
